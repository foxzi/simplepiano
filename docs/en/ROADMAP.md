# Roadmap

> **Note:** this document describes the **desired and planned curriculum** —
> the direction the project is aiming for. It is **not** a list of already
> implemented features. Today the app implements only a small part of what
> is listed below (see [README.md](./README.md), "Limitations" section).
> The items below are a development plan, not a claim of readiness.

## Full curriculum (48 topics)

The order and numbering of the topics below is fixed and must not change in
future edits of this document.

1. Getting to know the keyboard
2. Finding the note C
3. Names of the white keys
4. Octaves
5. Finger numbers
6. Five notes with the right hand
7. Five notes with the left hand
8. Simple note sequences
9. Rhythm basics
10. Playing with a metronome
11. Quarter, half, and whole notes
12. Eighth notes and rests
13. Introduction to the musical staff
14. Treble clef
15. Reading notes with the right hand
16. Bass clef
17. Reading notes with the left hand
18. Simple melodies from notation
19. C major scale — right hand
20. C major scale — left hand
21. C major scale — both hands
22. Sharps and flats
23. G major scale
24. F major scale
25. D major scale
26. The remaining major scales
27. Whole tone and semitone
28. Intervals
29. Intervals by ear
30. Major triads
31. Minor triads
32. Chord inversions
33. Chords C, F, and G
34. The I–IV–V–I progression
35. Melody in the right hand + bass in the left
36. Melody in the right hand + chords in the left
37. Playing with both hands
38. Simple pieces
39. Sight reading
40. Playing without hints
41. Playing at a set tempo
42. Gradually increasing BPM
43. Minor scales
44. Arpeggios
45. More complex rhythms
46. Dynamics and musical expression
47. The pedal
48. Final beginner-level pieces

## Status

The current prototype includes a one-octave C major scale exercise with
hand selection; this is a foundation for topics 19–20, not a complete course.
This list describes the intended curriculum, not a list of completed lessons.
See [README.md](./README.md) for the prototype's capabilities.
